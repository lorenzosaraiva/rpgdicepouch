Contact the developer

RPG Dice Pouch is developed by Lorenzo Saraiva. Describe the problem and, if possible, include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots are helpful when they do not contain sensitive information.

For purchase issues, tell us whether you used the App Store or Google Play and include the product name and order or transaction ID if available. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or full payment card details.

Email support

Restore purchases

In a version with purchases enabled, connect to the internet and sign in to the same App Store or Google Play account used for the purchase. Open Profile → Subscription → Restore purchases in RPG Dice Pouch.

Restoring checks for existing purchases; it does not make a new purchase. If access is still missing, contact support before buying again. Do not assume a purchase made on one platform transfers to the other: cross-platform access requires a supported shared app account.

If the app says purchases or plans are unavailable, your version or storefront may not have purchases configured yet. Core dice rolling, local presets, and history remain available without a purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

If you have a recurring subscription, use Profile → Subscription → Manage subscription when available. You can also manage it directly through the store where you subscribed:

  • iPhone: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RPG Dice Pouch. Apple cancellation instructions.
  • Android: open Google Play → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → RPG Dice Pouch. Google Play cancellation instructions.

Deleting the app, clearing local data, or deleting an app account does not cancel a subscription. A one-time cosmetic or lifetime purchase has no recurring renewal to cancel. Refund requests are handled under the store's policies: request an Apple refund or read Google Play refund instructions.

Privacy and data deletion

Read our Privacy Policy and data and account deletion instructions. Local data can be removed in Profile → Delete Local Data. If cloud accounts are enabled and you created one, account deletion is available in Account Settings.

For a privacy or deletion request without the app, email lorenzo.saraiva@hotmail.com. Mention whether your request concerns a cloud account or purchase-related data. We may need to verify ownership. Store transaction records may be retained by the payment providers under their policies and legal obligations.

Restaurar compras

Em uma versão com compras habilitadas, conecte-se à internet e utilize a mesma conta da App Store ou do Google Play usada na compra. No app, abra Perfil → Assinatura → Restaurar compras. Isso verifica compras existentes e não faz uma nova compra. Se o acesso não voltar, fale com o suporte antes de comprar novamente. Compras entre plataformas dependem de uma conta compartilhada compatível.

Cancelar uma assinatura

Se houver uma assinatura recorrente, use Perfil → Assinatura → Gerenciar assinatura quando disponível. No iPhone, acesse Ajustes → seu nome → Assinaturas. No Android, abra Google Play → ícone do perfil → Pagamentos e assinaturas → Assinaturas. Excluir o app ou uma conta não cancela a assinatura. Uma compra vitalícia ou de um cosmético não tem renovação recorrente.

Excluir dados

No app, use Perfil → Excluir dados locais. Se as contas na nuvem estiverem habilitadas e você tiver criado uma, poderá excluí-la nas configurações da conta. Consulte as instruções de exclusão ou solicite ajuda pelo e-mail acima. Excluir dados não cancela assinaturas nem necessariamente apaga os registros mantidos pelas lojas.
