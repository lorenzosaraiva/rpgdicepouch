Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

RPG Dice Pouch is developed by Lorenzo Saraiva. This policy explains how the app handles information when you roll dice, save presets, personalize the app, or use optional online features.

At a glance

Core dice tools work offline without an account. Your rolls, presets, and preferences are saved on your device. Optional accounts sync data to the cloud, and optional purchases use the app store and RevenueCat. We do not sell your personal information or use advertising or tracking SDKs.

1. Information we handle

Data on your device

The app saves dice selections, roll results and history, Arsenal presets, an optional display name, language, appearance settings, and purchase access information in local app storage. This lets the app remember your choices between sessions. Core rolling tools do not send your rolls or presets to us when you use the app without a cloud account.

Optional cloud accounts

In versions where cloud accounts are enabled, choosing to register or sign in provides your email address, name, authentication information, and account identifier to our account service, powered by Supabase. Authentication session information is also stored on your device to keep you signed in.

While you are signed in, your profile, dice settings, roll history, presets, and preferences are synced to your account so they can be restored on another device. You can use the core app without creating an account.

Optional purchases

In versions where purchases are enabled, the app store and RevenueCat process purchase and subscription information, such as product identifiers, transaction records, purchase dates, and entitlement status. RevenueCat uses an app user identifier, which can be linked to your account when you sign in, and may process device, app, and network information to validate purchases and provide its service. We use this information to unlock content, check subscriptions, restore purchases, and understand purchase and subscription activity through RevenueCat's reporting.

Payments are handled by Google Play or Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Privacy and support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond to your request, resolve the issue, or verify and carry out a data request. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide the features you choose: saving app state, managing and authenticating accounts, syncing data, fulfilling purchases, restoring access, and answering requests. Service providers may also process technical logs, including IP addresses, to operate and secure these services.

The app does not include advertising SDKs or dedicated behavioral analytics SDKs. It does not access your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or personal photo library. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

3. Service providers and sharing

Information is shared with the providers needed for the optional features you use:

  • Supabase: account authentication and cloud storage, when cloud accounts are enabled. Supabase privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat: purchase validation, subscriptions, and access to purchased content, when purchases are enabled. RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Google Play or Apple: payments, subscriptions, and store transaction records. Google privacy policy and Apple privacy policy.

We may disclose information where necessary to comply with a legal obligation or protect the security of the service. Online providers may process information in countries other than your own.

4. Storage, security, and retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or clear the app's storage. The Android app disables automatic app-data backup. Online account and sync connections use HTTPS, and cloud data access is restricted to the authenticated account. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Cloud account information and synced app data are kept while your account exists and removed from the active account service when your account is deleted. Local data must be removed separately. Purchase providers may retain transaction records under their own retention policies and legal obligations; deleting an app account does not erase store transaction records.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations. Technical logs and any provider backups follow the relevant provider's retention and deletion processes.

5. Delete your data or account

Remove data from your device

In the app, open Profile → Delete Local Data and confirm. This resets your local profile, roll history, presets, preferences, and locally cached purchase access. You can also clear the app's storage in Android Settings. Clearing local data does not delete a cloud account or cancel a subscription.

Delete a cloud account

If you have created a cloud account, open Profile → Account Settings → Delete account and confirm. This deletes your cloud account and associated synced profile and app data. Data already stored on your device remains until you delete it separately.

Request deletion without the app

Email lorenzo.saraiva@hotmail.com with the subject RPG Dice Pouch data deletion request. Tell us the email address associated with your account and whether you want to delete the account or request deletion of specific data. We may need to verify account ownership before proceeding. Do not include your password.

If your request concerns RevenueCat purchase-related data and you do not have a cloud account, tell us which store you used and provide an order or transaction ID if available so we can help identify the relevant record. Do not send full payment card details. Deleting local data does not delete RevenueCat purchase records; contact us to request assistance.

We cannot remotely delete data held only on your device. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play or the App Store; account or local-data deletion does not cancel them.
